FAQ

I'm running into StackOverFlowError, OutOfMemoryError or seeing that my assessment timed out​

on one of the tests.

If you're receiving a StackOverFlowError, it might imply that there is an infinite recursion happening somewhere in your implementation. Double check that you're accounting for different cases correctly - for example, what should happen if you try to union two vertices within the same set?
