The Hanota problem (Tower of Hanoi) is a famous example of a divide-and-conquer algorithm! 
- The goal is to move $n$ disks from one rod to another, using a third rod as temporary storage. You can move only one disk at a time, and a larger disk can never sit on a smaller disk.

![[Pasted image 20260923171641.png|500]]


To solve it, we break the problem into three steps:

1. Move the top $n-1$ disks from the starting rod to the temporary rod.
2. Move the largest disk to the destination rod.
3. Move the $n-1$ disks from the temporary rod to the destination rod.
