The N-Queens problem is a classic example of using backtracking: Given $n$ queens and an $n \cdot n$ chessboard, find an arrangement such that no two queens can attack each other.

![[Pasted image 20260923171525.png|500]]

The idea is that we place queens one at a time, and whenever no valid placement remains, undo an earlier placement and try another position.
